How It Works

1. Select Network & Amount

Choose your network (MTN, Airtel, Glo or 9mobile) and enter the amount of airtime you want to convert.

2. Enter Phone Number

Enter the phone number that currently has the airtime.

3. Transfer the Airtime

Follow the simple instructions to transfer the airtime securely.

4. Receive Your Cash

After successful conversion, the cash equivalent will be sent to your bank account.
